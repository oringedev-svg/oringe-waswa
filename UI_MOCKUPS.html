🎨 Design Principles in Action

🧠 Cognitive Psychology

  • Clear hierarchy reduces decision paralysis
  • Status badges show at a glance
  • Action buttons always visible
  • No surprise modals or distractions

💚 Health & Wellness

  • 1.6 line-height reduces eye strain
  • Blue-based palette aids focus
  • Dark mode available for evening work
  • No flashing/jarring animations

⚡ Productivity

  • Prioritized task list (urgent first)
  • One-click actions (no sub-menus)
  • Keyboard shortcuts throughout
  • Flow state design (no interruptions)

🌍 Global Design

  • Icon + text (works across languages)
  • Culturally neutral color choices
  • WCAG AAA accessibility
  • Works on mobile (40% of users)
